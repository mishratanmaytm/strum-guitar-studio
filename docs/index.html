What's inside

🎯 Tuner

Listens through the mic and shows how sharp or flat you are. Six tunings, tap-to-hear reference notes and A4 calibration.

🥁 Metronome

30 to 300 BPM, six time signatures, eighth-note clicks and tap tempo. Timed with the audio clock, so it doesn't drift.

🎸 Chords

Seven chord types in all 12 keys, with open and barre shapes, finger diagrams, and a button to hear them strummed.

📈 Practice log

A timer, a daily goal, a streak counter and a weekly chart. Everything stays on your device.

Install on Android

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Android app (APK).
  2. Tap the downloaded file Strum.apk. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Tap Install, then Open. When Strum asks to use the microphone for the tuner, tap Allow.
Why does Android show a warning?

Strum isn't on the Play Store yet, so Android shows a caution for any app installed from a website. Only install apps from people you trust.

Does Strum record or send my audio anywhere?

No. The tuner works out the pitch live on your device and throws the sound away. Strum has no accounts and no internet features, and your practice log stays on your phone.

iPhone?

There's no iPhone app, but Open in browser works in Safari. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen to use it like an app.

Updating the app

If a new version doesn't install over the old one, uninstall Strum first and install the new file. Your practice log lives inside the app, so uninstalling erases it.
